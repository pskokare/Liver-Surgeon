Liver Transplant Recovery Phases

Immediate Post-Transplant Phase (0–7 Days)

  • Stay in ICU for monitoring.
  • Pain and breathing support ongoing.
  • Start immunosuppressants.
  • Gradual tube and drain removal.
  • Oral fluids by Day 3–4.
✅ Goal: Stabilize and prevent infection

Hospital Stay (1–3 Weeks)

  • 2–3 weeks average stay.
  • Physiotherapy begins.
  • Start liver-friendly diet.
  • Care training for family.
✅ Goal: Prepare for discharge

First 3 Months Post-Discharge

  • Weekly checkups, then biweekly.
  • Blood tests and dose adjustments.
  • Light daily activities allowed.
✅ Goal: Regain independence

3 to 6 Months Post-Transplant

  • Monthly visits.
  • Light work and yoga possible.
  • Improved stamina.
✅ Goal: Resume physical activity

6 Months to 1 Year

  • Reduced medications.
  • Cleared for travel & sports.
✅ Goal: Rebuild strength and freedom

Beyond 1 Year

  • Lifelong follow-up.
  • Healthy lifestyle, avoid alcohol.
✅ Goal: Maintain long-term wellness
