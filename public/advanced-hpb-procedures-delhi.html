Advanced HPB Surgical Techniques

Ante-Situm & Ex-Situ Liver Resection

For ultra-complex liver tumors involving major veins. The liver is partially/fully removed, tumors resected on the bench and reimplanted. Curative option for otherwise inoperable tumors.

Portal Hypertension Surgery

TIPS (Transjugular Intrahepatic Portosystemic Shunt) reduces vein pressure to prevent bleeding. Devascularization controls variceal bleeding in cirrhosis cases.

Read More

Bile Duct Injury & Reconstruction

Post-cholecystectomy repairs with hepaticojejunostomy to restore bile flow. Stricture management through balloon dilation or stenting procedures.

Read More

Chronic Pancreatitis Surgery

Frey Procedure drains pancreatic ducts and removes scar tissue. Pancreaticojejunostomy relieves pain and improves digestion in chronic cases.

Minimal Access Liver Surgery
