What is a Split Liver Transplant?

A split liver transplant surgery is an advanced procedure where a deceased donor's liver is surgically divided into two functional grafts to save two patients – typically one adult and one child. This approach maximizes organ availability, addressing India's critical donor shortage. At Liver Surgeons, Delhi's premier transplant center, we specialize in complex split liver transplantation, balancing technical excellence with life-saving outcomes.

The Science of Split Liver Transplantation

Anatomical Precision

The donor's liver is surgically divided into:

  • Left Lateral Lobe (20%): Transplanted into a child
  • Right Lobe (80%): Transplanted into an adult

Liver Regeneration

Both segments regrow to full size in recipients within 6-8 weeks.

Ideal Candidates

  • Pediatric-Adult pairs: Children with biliary atresia + adults with cirrhosis
  • Two adults: Rare cases when liver size permits

Technical Challenges in Split Liver Transplants

1. Vascular & Biliary Anatomy

Splitting must preserve bile ducts and blood vessels for both grafts.

2. Graft Size Matching

Pediatric recipients require precise left lateral lobe volumes (200-300g).

3. Rapid Transplantation

Both grafts must be transplanted within 12-18 hours to ensure viability.
