Robotic Procedures

  1. Robotic-Assisted Liver Surgery
    • Technique: Removes liver tumors/cysts with 3D magnification and wristed instruments.
    • Benefits: Preserves healthy tissue; ideal for robotic liver cyst excision and tumors near critical vessels.
  2. Da Vinci HPB Procedures
    • Pancreaticoduodenectomy (Whipple Surgery): Removes pancreatic/duodenal tumors with robotic precision.
    • Bile Duct Reconstruction: Repairs strictures or injuries using microsuturing tools.
  3. Robotic Surgery for Gallbladder Cancer
    • Approach: Radical cholecystectomy with lymph node dissection, minimizing damage to surrounding organs.
  4. Reduced Blood Loss in Robotic Surgery
    • Advantage: The Da Vinci System's cautery and precision reduce intraoperative bleeding by 40-50%.
  5. Robotic Pancreaticoduodenectomy
    • Outcome: Shorter hospital stays (7-10 days) and lower complication rates vs. open surgery.
