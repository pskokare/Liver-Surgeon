How FibroScan Works

  • Stiffness Measurement: A painless probe sends vibrations through the liver, measuring wave speed to assess fibrosis (stiffness).
  • CAP Score: Controlled Attenuation Parameter quantifies fat percentage in the liver.
  • Duration: 10-15 minutes, with immediate results.

Why Choose FibroScan Over Liver Biopsy?

Key Applications of FibroScan

  • Fatty Liver (MASLD): CAP score tracks fat reduction during diet/weight loss.
  • Cirrhosis Monitoring: Detects early progression to guide treatment.
  • Post-Hepatitis Care: Assesses fibrosis regression after antiviral therapy.
  • Pre-Transplant Evaluation: Safe for patients with bleeding disorders.
