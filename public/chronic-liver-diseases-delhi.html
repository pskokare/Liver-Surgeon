What is Chronic Liver Disease (CLD)?

Chronic Liver Disease is a progressive situation where long-term damage (from alcohol, viruses, or fat) leads to liver scarring (fibrosis), cirrhosis, and eventual organ failure. At Liver Surgeons, Delhi’s premier CLD treatment center, we focus on slowing disease progression, managing complications, and improving quality of life.

  • Alcohol: Heavy drinking damages liver cells, causing inflammation and fibrosis.
  • Hepatitis B/C: Chronic viral infections lead to cirrhosis over decades.
  • NAFLD/NASH: Fat buildup in the liver due to obesity/diabetes.
  • Autoimmune/Genetic Disorders: Conditions like autoimmune hepatitis or Wilson’s disease.

Symptoms of CLD

Early detection saves lives. Watch for:

  • Persistent fatigue or weakness
  • Jaundice (yellow skin/eyes)
  • Swollen legs/abdomen (edema/ascites)
  • Easy bruising/bleeding
  • Confusion (hepatic encephalopathy)

Advanced Diagnosis

Liver Fibrosis Staging

  • FibroScan®: Non-invasive test to measure liver stiffness (stage F0-F4).
  • MRI Elastography: Detects early fibrosis in NAFLD patients.
  • Biopsy: Confirms cirrhosis or rules out cancer.

Portal Hypertension Evaluation

  • Endoscopy: Checks for esophageal varices (swollen veins).

CT Angiography: Maps blood flow abnormalities.

Chronic Liver Disease Treatment

1. Lifestyle Modifications

  • Alcohol Cessation: Critical for alcoholic CLD.
  • Weight Management: 5–10% weight loss reverses early NAFLD.
  • Low-Sodium Diet: Reduces ascites and swelling.

2. Medical Management

  • Antivirals: Tenofovir (hepatitis B), DAAs (hepatitis C).
  • Diuretics: Manage fluid retention (e.g., spironolactone).
  • Beta-Blockers: Prevent variceal bleeding (propranolol).

3. Advanced Interventions

  • TIPS Procedure: Reduces portal hypertension and bleeding risks.
  • Liver Transplant: For end-stage liver disease (MELD score ≥15).
