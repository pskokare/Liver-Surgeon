What is Post-Transplant Surveillance?

Post-transplant surveillance is a structured, lifelong program to ensure the long-term success of your liver transplant. It includes regular follow-up care, rejection monitoring, and managing complications to keep your new liver healthy. Key Components of Our Follow-Up Care

1. Transplant Rejection Surveillance

  • Liver Biopsy After Transplant: Gold standard for detecting rejection (performed annually or if enzymes rise).
  • Blood Tests: Monthly ALT, AST, bilirubin, and immunosuppressant levels.

2. Managing Transplant Complications

  • Infections: Prophylactic antivirals/antibiotics for CMV, EBV, and fungal risks.
  • Metabolic Issues: Diabetes, hypertension, and kidney function monitoring.

3. Viral Reactivation Monitoring

  • Routine PCR tests for hepatitis B/C recurrence.
  • Preemptive therapy if viral loads increase.

4. Lifestyle After Liver Transplant

  • Diet Plans: Low-salt, low-sugar, and high-protein diets.
  • Fitness Guidance: Safe exercise routines to rebuild strength.
  • Mental Health: Counseling for anxiety or post-surgery trauma.

5. Follow-Up After Donor Surgery

  • Donors receive 5-year check-ups for liver regeneration and overall health.

Our Post-Transplant Surveillance Process

Phase 1: First 3 Months

  • Weekly Visits: Bloodwork, drug levels, and wound checks.
  • Education: Managing medications, infection signs, and diet.

Phase 2: 3–12 Months

  • Bi-Monthly Visits: Ultrasound + FibroScan to assess liver stiffness.
  • Immunosuppression Adjustments: Minimize side effects (e.g., tremors, hair loss).

Phase 3: 1–5 Years

  • Quarterly Visits: Bone density scans and kidney function tests.
  • Cancer Screening: Skin, colon, and liver cancer checks.

Phase 4: Beyond 5 Years

Annual Full-Body Health Review: Cardiac, metabolic, and mental wellness.
