Metabolic Liver Diseases

What Are Metabolic Liver Diseases?

Metabolic liver diseases are genetic or acquired disorders disrupting the liver’s ability to process proteins, fats, or carbohydrates. Key conditions we treat:

  • Glycogen Storage Diseases (GSD): Inability to break down glycogen, causing hypoglycemia and growth delays.
  • Urea Cycle Disorders: Buildup of ammonia (hyperammonemia), leading to brain damage.
  • Gilbert’s Syndrome: Mild bilirubin metabolism issues causing intermittent jaundice.
  • Lipid Metabolism Disorders: Abnormal fat processing, leading to liver steatosis or cardiomyopathy.

Early intervention prevents irreversible organ damage and supports normal development in children.

Symptoms & Diagnosis

Common Symptoms

  • Pediatric: Poor growth, hypoglycemia, developmental delays.
  • Adults: Fatigue, jaundice, confusion (due to hyperammonemia).

Advanced Diagnostics

  • Blood Tests: Ammonia levels, lactate, amino acid profiles.
  • Genetic Testing: Identifies mutations in G6PC (GSD-I) or OTC (urea cycle disorders).

Liver Biopsy: Confirms glycogen or fat accumulation.

Metabolic Liver Disease Treatment

1. Glycogen Storage Disease Care

  • Cornstarch Therapy: Frequent doses to maintain blood sugar.
  • Gene Therapy Trials: For GSD-Ia and Ib (available at our Delhi center).

2. Hyperammonemia Treatment

  • Ammonia Scavengers: Sodium benzoate/phenylbutyrate to lower ammonia.
  • Dialysis: For acute crises (ammonia >200 µmol/L).

3. Dietary Management for Metabolic Diseases

  • Low-Protein Diets: For urea cycle disorders (limits ammonia production).
  • High-Carbohydrate Meals: For GSD to prevent hypoglycemia.

4. Gilbert’s Syndrome Management

  • Reassurance and hydration; no active treatment needed.
