What is a Living Donor Liver Transplant?

A Living Donor Liver Transplant (LDLT) is a revolutionary procedure where a portion of a healthy person’s liver is transplanted into a patient with end-stage liver disease. At Liver Surgeons, we specialize in family donor liver transplants, where a relative’s liver segment (usually 20-25%) regenerates in both donor and recipient within weeks. This approach eliminates long organ waitlist times, offering a faster, life-saving solution.

Living Donor Liver Transplant (LDLT)

Life-saving transplants through ethical living donation with 95% success rates

Why Choose Living Donor Liver Transplant?

LDLT is the optimal solution for children and adults who cannot afford to wait for deceased donors, offering superior outcomes through immediate transplantation from healthy living donors.

Key Advantages

  • 95% success rates at our Delhi and Gurgaon centers, surpassing national averages
  • Ethical & Safe: Rigorous donor evaluation ensures no compromise on donor health
  • Ideal for pediatric patients: Allows transplantation before irreversible complications develop

Benefits of Living Donor Transplants

  • No Waitlist Delays: Bypass years of waiting with a pre-identified donor
  • Optimal Organ Quality: Healthy livers from living donors ensure better outcomes
  • Minimally Invasive Liver Donation: Laparoscopic techniques reduce donor pain and recovery time
  • Faster Recipient Recovery: Transplant timing can be planned electively
  • Cost-Effective: Lower risk of complications reduces long-term expenses

4-6 weeks

Average time from evaluation to transplant (vs. 12+ months wait for deceased donors)

The Living Donor Evaluation Process

Step 1: Initial Screening

  • Blood type compatibility (ABO matching)
  • Preliminary health checks (BMI, liver function tests)

Step 2: Comprehensive Medical Tests

  • Imaging: MRI/CT scans to assess liver volume and anatomy
  • Psychosocial Assessment: Ensure the donor's mental readiness and voluntary consent

Step 3: Final Approval

  • A multidisciplinary team, including hepatologists and ethicists, reviews all results
  • Only 1 in 4 donors qualify, prioritizing safety above all

Why Trust Our Evaluation Process?

  • Transparent Process: 100+ donors evaluated monthly at our Gurgaon facility
  • Zero Cost to Donors: All evaluations covered under recipient's package

Advanced Surgical Techniques

a. Minimally Invasive Liver Donation

Laparoscopic Liver Donor Surgery: 3-4 small incisions instead of large open cut

Benefits for Donors:

  • 50% less pain
  • 4-day hospital stay (vs. 7-10 days for open surgery)
  • Return to work in 3-4 weeks

b. Precision Transplant Surgery

Our Delhi team, led by Dr. Ashish George (15+ years' experience), uses 3D imaging to map liver segments for perfect graft-recipient fit.

Risks of Living Liver Donation

While LDLT is safe, we ensure complete transparency about potential risks:

Potential Risks

  • Surgical Risks: Bleeding, infection (1-2% cases)
  • Long-Term Concerns: Rare bile leakage or hernias

Our Safety Protocols

  • Robotic Assistance: Minimizes human error in laparoscopic surgeries
  • Post-Donation Care: Free follow-ups for donors for 2 years
  • Donor Advocate: Independent physician protects donor interests
