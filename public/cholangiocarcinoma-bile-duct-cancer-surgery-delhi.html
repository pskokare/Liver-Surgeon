Surgical Procedures

  1. Cholangiocarcinoma Resection
    • Procedure: Complete removal of bile duct tumors, often with liver or pancreatic segments.
    • Types:
      • Perihilar Bile Duct Cancer Surgery (Klatskin Tumor): Complex resection of tumors at the liver hilum.
      • Distal Bile Duct Resection: For tumors near the pancreas.
    • Radical Bile Duct Cancer Treatment
      • Technique: En-bloc removal of tumors with surrounding lymph nodes (lymphadenectomy).
      • Goal: Achieve margin-negative resection to prevent recurrence.
    • Palliative Biliary Drainage Surgery
      • Options: Surgical bypass or stenting to relieve bile flow obstruction and jaundice.
    • Adjuvant Therapy Integration
      • Post-Surgery Care: Chemotherapy or radiation to target residual cancer cells.
