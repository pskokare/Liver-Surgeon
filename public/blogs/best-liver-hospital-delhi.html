Best Liver Hospital in Delhi 2026: Top Centres for Treatment, Surgery & Transplant

Medically Reviewed by
Dr Ashish George
Senior Liver Transplant & HPB Surgeon with 15+ years of clinical expertise.

27 May 2026

Delhi is home to some of India's most advanced liver treatment centres, ranging from subsidised government institutions to highly equipped private hospitals. Whether you are dealing with fatty liver, hepatitis, liver cirrhosis, liver cancer, or end-stage liver disease requiring a transplant, the right hospital can make a significant difference in your outcome.


This guide provides a practical overview of the top liver hospitals in Delhi, what each one does well, who each one is best suited for, and what to expect in terms of costs and waiting times. The aim is to help patients and families make an informed choice rather than a rushed one.


What Makes a Good Liver Hospital?

Not all hospitals that treat liver disease are equipped to handle all levels of severity. Before choosing a centre, it helps to know what separates a general hospital with a gastroenterology unit from a dedicated liver centre. Key factors to evaluate:

  • Dedicated hepatology and liver surgery unit: A standalone department focused exclusively on liver disease, not a shared gastroenterology unit handling all digestive conditions
  • Transplant capability: If there is any possibility that a transplant may be needed, it is worth choosing a centre that can evaluate, list, and operate without requiring a referral transfer
  • Liver ICU: Dedicated intensive care for liver patients is clinically distinct from a general ICU and matters significantly for complex cases and post-transplant recovery
  • Diagnostic depth: FibroScan, 3D CT volumetry, MR elastography, advanced liver biopsy capabilities, and precision oncology workup all indicate a centre serious about liver care.
  • Case volume: A higher volume of liver cases translates to a more experienced multidisciplinary team and better-honed protocols
  • Supportive care: Liver disease patients often need nutritional support, physiotherapy, psychological counselling, and palliative care alongside their clinical treatment


Top Liver Hospitals in Delhi: Compared


Hospital Profiles

Fortis Hospital, Shalimar Bagh

Fortis Shalimar Bagh has a dedicated Liver Transplant and HPB Surgery unit that handles the full spectrum of liver care, from medical management of hepatitis and cirrhosis to complex surgical procedures, including living donor, deceased donor, pediatric, ABO-incompatible, and combined liver-kidney transplants. The unit features a hybrid operating theatre with real-time imaging, a dedicated liver ICU, and a robotic surgical platform.

The program is led by a team with collectively over 1,000 liver transplant surgeries performed. It accepts patients from across India and internationally, with coordination support for outstation patients. The hospital is NABH-accredited and has treated patients from the Middle East, South Asia, and Africa. Its cost range of ₹22 to ₹35 lakh for a living donor transplant puts it at the mid-to-upper range of Delhi private hospitals, with lower waiting times than government centres.

AIIMS Delhi

The All India Institute of Medical Sciences remains India's most recognised government hospital for liver disease and transplant. Its liver transplant program has trained a significant portion of India's transplant surgeons and continues to handle a wide range of complex cases at subsidised rates of ₹6 to ₹15 lakh. The hospital is the best option for patients who need the lowest possible cost and have the medical stability to wait for a slot.

The primary limitation of AIIMS is the waiting time. The demand for treatment at subsidised government rates is enormous. Patients in acute liver failure or with rapidly deteriorating MELD scores may not be able to wait. AIIMS is also a teaching hospital, which means that procedures may involve supervised junior doctors to a greater extent than at private centres.

ILBS Delhi

The Institute of Liver and Biliary Sciences is Asia's only dedicated government liver hospital. It handles one of the highest volumes of liver transplants in India and specialises in complex hepatobiliary conditions, including pediatric liver disease, biliary atresia, metabolic liver disorders, and portal hypertension. Its cost structure at ₹15 to ₹30 lakh is more accessible than top private hospitals while maintaining specialised expertise.

ILBS accepts referrals from across India and runs both living donor and deceased donor programs. It has a formal organ sharing arrangement and is a recognised transplant centre under the National Organ & Tissue Transplant Organisation (NOTTO).

Max Hospital, Saket

Max Saket has one of Delhi's busiest private liver transplant programs, with over 2,600 transplants performed under its senior team. It offers a comprehensive liver care program with advanced imaging, a dedicated liver ICU, and a strong hepatology support team. Pricing at ₹23 to ₹40 lakh is at the upper end of Delhi's private market, and the hospital has a strong track record with complex cases.

Apollo Delhi

Apollo's Delhi facility offers liver transplant services within the broader Apollo Hospitals network, which has multiple liver transplant centres across India. For patients who may need to split their care between Delhi and another city, Apollo's pan-India network is a practical consideration. Cost ranges of ₹22 to ₹30 lakh are broadly comparable with those of other major private Delhi hospitals.

Sir Ganga Ram Hospital

Sir Ganga Ram Hospital is a respected private hospital in central Delhi with an active liver transplant and hepatobiliary surgery program. Its pricing in the range of ₹12 to ₹20 lakh makes it one of the more accessible private options in Delhi. It handles standard adult living donor and deceased donor transplants and is well-regarded for its hepatology medical management.

Medanta, Gurugram

While technically in Gurugram rather than Delhi, Medanta is within easy reach of the Delhi NCR and runs one of India's highest-volume private liver transplant programs. Costs range from ₹25 to ₹40 lakh. It is led by an extremely experienced transplant team and is a strong choice for patients who want maximum volume at a private centre and are willing to travel slightly outside Delhi city.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best liver hospital in Delhi for liver cirrhosis?

For medical management of liver cirrhosis without an immediate need for transplant, ILBS Delhi and Fortis Shalimar Bagh are both strong choices with dedicated hepatology teams. For patients who may progress to transplant, choosing a centre with an integrated transplant program from the start avoids delays in transitioning care.

Can I get a liver transplant at a government hospital in Delhi without being a Delhi resident?

Yes. Both AIIMS and ILBS treat patients from across India. There is no residency requirement. However, you will need to arrange accommodation near the hospital for the pre-transplant evaluation period and the extended post-transplant follow-up phase.

Does ILBS accept private patients or only government scheme patients?

ILBS treats patients under both government schemes and at self-pay rates. The pricing is still significantly lower than that of private hospitals, even at self-pay. Contact ILBS directly to understand the current admission processes for outstation patients.

What should I bring to a first consultation at a liver hospital in Delhi?

Bring all existing medical records, including liver function test reports, ultrasound or CT scans, biopsy reports if available, a list of current medications, and the referral letter from your treating doctor. The more complete your records, the more productive the first consultation.
