Success Rate of Liver Transplant in India: A Complete Patient Guide

Medically Reviewed by
Dr Ashish George
Senior Liver Transplant & HPB Surgeon with 15+ years of clinical expertise.

30 Mar 2026

A liver transplant is the way to save the life of a person with a very bad liver disease. This operation can give people hope and a new start. It also makes us think about the risks and how well it works.


At Liver Surgeons we know that patients and their families need to have trustworthy information before making such a big decision. This guide will tell you everything you need to know about the risks and success rates of liver transplants in India. It will also help you take the step with confidence.


What Is a Liver Transplant?

A liver transplant is an operation where a bad liver is replaced with one from a donor. There are two types of liver transplants:

Living Donor Liver Transplant: 

A part of the liver is given by a living person

Deceased Donor Liver Transplant: 

A whole liver is taken from a person who's brain dead


When Do You Need a Liver Transplant?

You need a liver transplant when your liver is not working properly. Some common conditions that may need a liver transplant are:

  • Liver cirrhosis
  • Chronic hepatitis B or C
  • Acute liver failure
  • Liver cancer
  • Advanced fatty liver disease

Success Rate of Liver Transplant in India

India is known for its good liver transplant operations, experienced surgeons and affordable treatment.

The success rates are:

  • 1-year survival rate: 85% to 90%
  • 5-year survival rate: 70% to 75%

Living donor transplant success rate: higher because the operation is planned and the donor is a good match

These results are as good as those in countries.

What Factors Affect Liver Transplant Success?


Understanding these factors can help improve results and reduce problems.

1. The patient's health: 

Patients who are diagnosed early and treated on time tend to do better.


2. Donor match quality: 

A good donor match can make the transplant more successful.


3. The expertise of the team: 

Choosing an experienced liver surgeon in Delhi is important for minimizing risks.


4. Post-transplant care: taking medications, going to follow-ups and making lifestyle changes can ensure long-term success.


Risks of Liver Transplant

Although liver transplantation is an advanced operation it does have some risks. Knowing about these risks can help patients prepare.


Surgical Risks:

1. Bleeding: 

The liver has a lot of blood which can make bleeding a problem during the operation.

2. Infection: 

infections can happen after the operation. They can be treated with good medical care.

3. Blood Clots: 

clots can form in the blood vessels connected to the liver.

Post-Transplant Risks:

4. Organ Rejection: the immune system may reject the liver but this can be controlled with medications.

5. Bile Duct Complications: problems with bile flow can happen. They can be treated if caught early.

6. Side Effects of Medications: long-term use of medications can lead to:

  • blood pressure
  • Diabetes
  • Kidney problems

Long-Term Risks:

  • The original liver disease can come back
  • Lifestyle-related health issues can happen
  • Patients need to be monitored and take medications for the rest of their lives

Frequently Asked Questions

  1. What is the success rate of liver transplant in India?

The success rate is between 85% to 90% for the year depending on the patient's health and care.


  1. Is liver transplant a high-risk surgery?

It is an operation but the risks can be managed with experienced surgeons and good care.


  1. Can patients live a life after transplant?

Yes, most patients can go back to their lives after recovery.


  1. How does a transplanted liver last?

A transplanted liver can work for decades or even a lifetime with good care.
