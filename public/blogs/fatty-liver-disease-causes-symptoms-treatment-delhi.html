Fatty Liver Disease: Causes, Symptoms, and Best Treatment Options

Fatty Liver Disease: Causes, Symptoms, and Best Treatment Options

Medically Reviewed by
Dr Ashish George
Senior Liver Transplant & HPB Surgeon with 15+ years of clinical expertise.

03 Apr 2026

Fatty Liver Disease is emerging as one of the most common diseases affecting the liver, not only worldwide but also in the Indian population. This is largely because of the sedentary lifestyle, consumption of junk food, and the growing incidence of obesity and diabetes.

What is worrying about this disease is that the patient does not show any symptoms until the disease is in its advanced state.

At Liver Surgeons, we come across many patients who are not aware that they are suffering from the disease until they undergo routine blood tests. In this blog, you will be able to learn about the disease in detail.


What is Fatty Liver Disease?

Fatty Liver Disease is a medical condition where there is an accumulation of extra fat in the liver cells. Although the liver contains some amounts of fat in its cells, an increase in the amount of fat in the liver cells above 5 or 10 percent interferes with the functioning of the liver.


Types of Fatty Liver Disease


1. Non-Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (NAFLD)

Most common type of FLD

Occurs in people who do not take alcohol or who take very small amounts of alcohol

Closely related to lifestyle disorders


2. Alcoholic Fatty Liver Disease (AFLD)

Result of excessive alcohol intake

Leads to rapid deterioration of the liver if not treated properly


Why Fatty Liver is Increasing in India

In India, there is a sharp rise in Fatty Liver cases, which is attributed to:

  • High consumption of junk food
  • Increase in desk jobs
  • Increase in obesity and diabetes cases
  • Lack of proper sleep and high-stress levels

Young adults are also coming down with Fatty Liver, so awareness is a necessity.

Causes of Fatty Liver Disease

Normally, fatty liver is caused by a combination of several factors.

Primary Causes:

  • Being obese or overweight
  • Diabetes type 2 or insulin resistance
  • Elevated cholesterol and triglycerides
  • Poor diet
  • Alcohol intake


Secondary Causes:

  • Rapid weight loss
  • Imbalance of hormones
  • Certain medications
  • Genetic problems

It is important to know the cause in order to treat the disease effectively.


Symptoms of Fatty Liver Disease

Fatty liver is sometimes called a “silent condition” since its signs and symptoms may not be noticeable at the initial stages.


Early Signs:

  • Fatigue or low energy
  • Slight discomfort in the upper abdomen
  • Feeling bloated or heavy


Advanced Symptoms:

  • Sudden weight loss
  • Weakness
  • Jaundice or yellowing of skin/eyes
  • Swelling of legs or abdomen
  • Mental confusion


If you notice any of these signs or symptoms, it is advisable to see a specialist immediately if you are in a high-risk category.


Stages of Fatty Liver Disease

Knowing the different stages of fatty liver helps in early intervention:


Stage 1: Simple Fatty Liver

  • Fat deposition without inflammation
  • Completely reversible


Stage 2: NASH (Non-Alcoholic Steatohepatitis)

  • Inflammation of the fatty liver
  • Liver cells are damaged


Stage 3: Fibrosis

  • Scar tissue formation
  • Liver function gradually declines


Stage 4: Cirrhosis

  • Severe scarring
  • Liver failure or cancer can occur


Diagnosis of Fatty Liver Disease

Doctors use different methods to diagnose fatty liver disease:

  • Liver function blood tests
  • Ultrasound scan
  • FibroScan (non-invasive liver stiffness test)
  • CT or MRI scans
  • Liver biopsy in complex cases

Regular health checkups are important for early detection.


Best Treatment Options for Fatty Liver Disease

There is no medication that completely cures fatty liver. The focus is on lifestyle modifications and medical management.


1. Diet and Nutrition

Healthy nutrition is the first step in the treatment of fatty liver.

Recommended Foods:

  • Green vegetables
  • Fruits
  • Whole grains
  • Lean proteins
  • Healthy fats

Foods to Avoid:

  • Sugary drinks
  • Fried foods
  • Fast foods
  • Processed foods
  • Refined carbohydrates


2. Exercise and Physical Activity

Regular exercise is beneficial in burning liver fat. It also improves metabolism.

  • 30-45 minutes of daily activity
  • Combination of cardio and strength training
  • Even walking is beneficial


3. Weight Loss

Weight loss of 5 to 10 percent can lead to a substantial reduction in liver fat.

  • Avoid Crash Diets
  • Gradual weight loss


4. Medical Treatment

Doctors can prescribe medicine to control:

  • Diabetes
  • Cholesterol
  • Liver Inflammation

Always follow experts’ advice before taking medicine.


5. Alcohol Avoidance

Avoid the intake of alcohol when diagnosed with fatty liver because it speeds up the progression of the condition.

Can Fatty Liver Be Reversed?

Yes, fatty liver is one of the few liver conditions that can be reversed in the early stages.


With:

  • Proper diet
  • Exercise
  • Weight management
  • Under medical supervision


The condition improves within a few months.

Complications of Untreated Fatty Liver

The complications of an untreated fatty liver disease include:


Liver Inflammation (NASH)

  • Fibrosis
  • Cirrhosis
  • Liver Failure
  • Liver Cancer


Fatty liver disease also increases the risk of:

  • Heart Diseases
  • Stroke
  • Metabolic Diseases

Prevention Tips for Fatty Liver Disease

Prevention is simple, yet it calls for consistency.


Healthy Habits:

  • Ideal Body Weight
  • Balanced Diet
  • Regular Exercise
  • No Alcohol
  • Diabetes and Cholesterol Management
  • Regular Health Check-Ups


When Should You See a Liver Specialist?

You should see a doctor in case:

  • You have diabetes or obesity
  • Liver tests are abnormal
  • You have persistent fatigue
  • You have a family history of liver disease


Final Thoughts

Fatty Liver Disease is not a serious disease in the early stages, yet it can become one if ignored. The good news is that with early detection and proper lifestyle changes, it is entirely manageable and can even be reversed.

Being proactive today can help your liver for tomorrow.

FAQs


1. Is fatty liver serious?

Yes, it can become serious if not treated, leading to cirrhosis or liver failure.


2. Can fatty liver be cured permanently?

Yes, it can be cured in early stages with proper lifestyle changes.


3. What is the best diet for fatty liver?

A diet with plenty of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, and lean proteins.


4. Do I need surgery for fatty liver?

No, most cases do not need surgery, but with proper attention from experts for serious cases
