Types of Hepatitis & Treatment

Hepatitis A

  • Cause: Contaminated food/water (HAV).
  • Prevention: Hepatitis A vaccination (2-dose schedule).
  • Treatment: Self-limiting, hydration, and rest.

Hepatitis B

  • Cause: Blood/body fluids (HBV).
  • Care:
    • Chronic Hepatitis Management: Antivirals like Tenofovir or Entecavir to suppress HBV.
    • Vertical Transmission Prevention: Vaccinate newborns within 12 hours + HBIG for HBV+ mothers.
  • Specialists: India’s top hepatitis B treatment specialists at our Delhi clinic.

Hepatitis C

  • Cause: Blood exposure (HCV).
  • Cure: Direct-acting antivirals (DAAs) like Sofosbuvir/Velpatasvir (12-week course, >95% cure rate).
  • Advanced Care: FibroScan® to assess liver damage from hepatitis.

Hepatitis D

  • Cause: Co-infection with HBV (HDV).
  • Treatment: Pegylated interferon therapy.
  • Focus: Hepatitis D co-infection care to prevent rapid cirrhosis.

Hepatitis E

  • Cause: Contaminated water (HEV).
  • Risk: Severe in pregnant women.

Prevention: Safe drinking water; avoid undercooked meat.

Advanced Diagnosis & Monitoring

  • HBV/HCV Viral Load Testing: Quantifies virus in the blood to guide therapy.
  • Liver Function Tests (LFTs): Tracks ALT/AST levels for liver inflammation.
  • FibroScan®: Stages fibrosis/cirrhosis caused by chronic hepatitis.

PCR Testing: Detects active HBV/HCV replication.
