Pre-Transplant Evaluation at Liver Surgeons


– Your First Step Toward a New Life

A liver transplant is a life-saving surgery, but success begins well before the operation. At Liver Surgeons, Delhi’s leading center for liver care, we follow a thorough Pre-Transplant Evaluation process to ensure each patient is physically, medically, emotionally, and financially ready for transplantation.

Book Appointment

Why is Pre-Transplant Evaluation Important?

  • To assess if you are a suitable candidate for a liver transplant.
  • To minimize risks and optimize your health before surgery.
  • To design a personalized treatment and recovery plan for the best possible outcomes.

The power of community to create health is far greater than any physician, clinic or hospital.

Key Post-Transplant Instructions You Must Follow

1. Medical History Review

  • We review your liver disease history, surgeries, and current medications.
  • We also evaluate comorbid conditions like diabetes and heart disease.
📋 Goal: Understand your full medical background

2. Physical Examination

  • General physical check-up to identify existing health risks.
  • Supports early detection of complications.
🩺 Goal: Assess overall physical fitness for surgery

3. Laboratory Tests

  • Blood Tests: CBC, LFT, KFT, coagulation profile
  • Viral Screening: Hepatitis B/C, HIV
🧪 Goal: Establish internal health baseline

4. Imaging Tests

  • Ultrasound, CT, or MRI assess:
  • Liver size, tumor presence, and blood flow patterns.
🖼️ Goal: Visualize liver condition and surgical anatomy

5. Cardiac & Pulmonary Evaluation

  • Heart Tests: ECG, 2D Echo, stress test
  • Lung Tests: Pulmonary function tests
❤️ Goal: Ensure safe response to surgery and anesthesia

6. Cancer Screening

  • We screen for any cancers outside the liver that may impact outcomes.
  • Helps rule out transplant contraindications.
🎗️ Goal: Confirm cancer-free status for eligibility

7. Psychological Assessment

  • Emotional readiness and mental health screening.
  • Support offered for anxiety, depression, or lifestyle change adaptation.
🧠 Goal: Prepare you mentally for transplant journey

8. Social & Financial Assessment

  • Assess family support and social caregivers.
  • Discuss cost planning and insurance options.
💼 Goal: Build a stable support and funding system

9. Nutritional Assessment

  • Dietician reviews your current nutrition and health status.
  • Customized pre-transplant diet is suggested.
🥦 Goal: Optimize body strength before surgery

Outcome of the Pre-Transplant Evaluation

  • Approved for Listing

    You may be cleared for listing for a liver transplant.

  • Requires Optimization

    You may need treatment for infections, improved nutrition, or heart condition management before approval.

  • Alternative Options

    If unsuitable, we'll discuss alternative treatment options with you.

💡

Patient Tips for a Successful Evaluation

  • 1
    Be open about your medical history, medication use, and lifestyle habits.
  • 2
    It is important to attend all scheduled appointments and to follow your doctor's recommendations.
  • 3
    Prepare mentally and involve your family in the process.
