What is Autoimmune Hepatitis (AIH)?

Autoimmune Hepatitis (AIH) is a chronic disorder where the immune system wrongly attacks liver cells, causing inflammation and scarring. Without timely AIH diagnosis and treatment, it can progress to cirrhosis or liver failure. Types of Jaundice

1. Pre-hepatic jaundice

  • Cause: Excessive red blood cell breakdown (e.g., hemolytic anemia).
  • Treatment: Address anemia or infections triggering hemolysis.

2. Hepatic Jaundice

  • Causes:
    • Liver Damage: Hepatitis, cirrhosis, or Gilbert’s syndrome (a benign genetic disorder).
    • Medications: Drug-induced liver injury.
  • Care: Bilirubin level management with medications, lifestyle changes, or liver support therapies.

3. Post-hepatic (Obstructive) Jaundice

  • Cause: Blocked bile ducts (gallstones, tumors, strictures).

Treatment: Obstructive jaundice surgery (ERCP, stenting, or gallbladder removal).

Symptoms of Jaundice

  • Yellow skin/eyes
  • Dark urine, pale stools
  • Fatigue, abdominal pain
  • Itching (pruritus)
  • Weight loss (in chronic cases)

Diagnosis & Treatment

Diagnosis

  • Blood Tests: Bilirubin (direct/indirect), LFTs, CBC.
  • Imaging: Ultrasound, MRCP, or CT scan to detect bile duct obstructions.
  • Liver Biopsy: For suspected cirrhosis or hepatitis.

Treatment

  • Liver-Related Jaundice:
    • Hepatitis/Cirrhosis: Antivirals, steroids, or transplant evaluation.
    • Gilbert’s Syndrome Treatment: Reassurance and symptom management (no cure needed).
  • Obstructive Jaundice:
    • ERCP: Removes gallstones or places stents.
    • Surgery: Whipple procedure for pancreatic tumors.
  • Neonatal Jaundice Care:
    • Phototherapy: Breaks down bilirubin in newborns.

Exchange Transfusion: For severe cases (bilirubin >25 mg/dL).
