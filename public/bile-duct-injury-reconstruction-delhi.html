What is Bile Duct Injury & Reconstruction?

Bile duct injuries, often caused by gallbladder surgery (cholecystectomy), infections, or trauma, can lead to severe complications like jaundice, leaks, or strictures. At Liver Surgeons, Delhi’s leading center, we offer life-restoring solutions for:

  • Post-cholecystectomy bile duct repair (e.g., iatrogenic injuries).
  • Benign biliary stricture treatment (chronic inflammation, scarring).
  • Bile duct leak surgery (acute or chronic leaks).
  • ERCP failure management when endoscopic interventions are unsuccessful.

Surgical Procedures

  1. Bile Duct Injury Repair Surgery
    • Procedure: Reconstruction of damaged bile ducts using microsurgical techniques.
    • Use Cases: Injuries during gallbladder surgery, trauma, or tumors.
  2. Biliary Stricture Reconstruction
    • Approach: Hepaticojejunostomy surgery (connecting bile ducts to the small intestine) or balloon dilatation.
    • Outcome: Restores bile flow and prevents liver damage.
  3. Post-Cholecystectomy Bile Duct Repair
    • Expertise: Correcting injuries from gallbladder removal, including Roux-en-Y hepaticojejunostomy for complex cases.
  4. Roux-en-Y Hepaticojejunostomy
    • Technique: Creates a bypass between the bile duct and jejunum using a Roux limb, ideal for high strictures or large defects.
  5. Benign Biliary Stricture Treatment
    • Options: Surgical reconstruction or stenting, tailored to stricture location and severity.
  6. Bile Duct Leak Surgery
    • Methods: Direct repair, T-tube placement, or diversion procedures.
  7. ERCP Failure Management
    • Surgical Rescue: When ERCP stenting fails, surgical reconstruction offers definitive repair.
