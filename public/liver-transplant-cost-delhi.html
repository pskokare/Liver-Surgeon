How Much Does a Liver Transplant Cost in India?

The cost of a liver transplant in India varies depending on several factors — the type of transplant, the complexity of the patient's case, the length of hospital stay, and the specific hospital and surgical team chosen. However, India offers world-class liver transplant surgery at costs that are a fraction of what the same procedure would cost in the USA, UK, or Singapore.

Insurance & Financial Assistance

Health Insurance Coverage

  • Many Indian health insurance policies now include liver transplant coverage — check your policy for the specific sum insured and sub-limits.
  • Corporate group health policies often have higher coverage limits — check with your HR department for policy particulars.
  • Pre-authorisation from your insurer is required before admission — our billing team will assist with all documentation.
  • Government employees may be covered under CGHS (Central Government Health Scheme).

Government Schemes

  • Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY): Covers liver transplant costs for eligible below-poverty-line patients up to ₹5 lakhs per family per year.
  • Chief Minister's health schemes in various states may provide additional support for transplant patients.
  • ECHS (Ex-Servicemen Contributory Health Scheme): Covers liver transplants for eligible defence personnel.

NRI & International Patients

  • International patients should contact us in advance at info@liversurgeons.com for a personalised cost estimate.
  • We provide detailed cost estimates in USD, GBP, AED, and other currencies on request.
  • Assistance available for medical visa application, accommodation arrangements, and airport transfers.

Our billing and patient coordination team is here to help you understand your insurance coverage, navigate government programs, and plan your finances before committing to treatment. We believe every patient deserves clear, honest pricing — and we uphold that in every case.

What Factors Affect the Final Cost?

  • Type of transplant: Living donor vs deceased donor vs combined organ transplants
  • Complexity of the recipient's case: MELD score, active complications, prior surgeries
  • Length of ICU and hospital stay: Varies based on post-operative course
  • Need for additional procedures: TIPS, endoscopy, dialysis, plasmapheresis
  • Immunosuppression requirements: Some patients need higher or more expensive regimens
  • Post-transplant complications: Rejection episodes or infections may extend stay and add cost
  • Donor surgery complexity: Anatomical variations may require more complex donor hepatectomy
