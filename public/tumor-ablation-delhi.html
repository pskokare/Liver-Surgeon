Procedures & Techniques

  1. Radiofrequency Ablation (RFA)
    • Process: A thin needle electrode delivers high-frequency currents to heat tumors (60-100°C).
    • Use Cases: Small hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) (≤3 cm), superficial tumors.
    • Benefits: Low complication rates, outpatient procedure.
  2. Microwave Ablation (MWA)
    • Process: Microwaves generate intense heat, ideal for larger tumors (≤5 cm) or near blood vessels.
    • Advantages: Faster, more uniform heat distribution than RFA.
  3. Image-Guided Liver Ablation
    • Technology: Real-time ultrasound, CT, or MRI ensures precise needle placement.
    • Accuracy: Destroys tumors while sparing healthy tissue.

RFA vs Microwave Ablation

Aspect RFA Microwave Ablation
Energy Source Electrical currents Microwaves
Tumor Size ≤3 cm ≤5 cm
Heat Penetration Moderate Deeper, faster
Best For Small HCC, precise lesions Larger tumors, vascular regions
