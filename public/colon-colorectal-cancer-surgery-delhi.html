Surgical Procedures

1. Colectomy (Partial/Total)

  • Partial Colectomy: Removes the cancerous colon segment, reconnecting healthy ends
  • Total Colectomy: For widespread tumors or Lynch syndrome requiring ileostomy/colostomy

2. Low Anterior Resection (LAR)

  • Purpose: Removes rectal tumors while preserving the anal sphincter
  • Technique: Open, laparoscopic, or robotic Surgery
  • Recovery: 4–6 weeks with temporary ostomy in some cases

3. Laparoscopic Colon Surgery

  • Benefits: Smaller incisions, faster recovery (2–4 weeks), and reduced pain
  • Ideal For: Early-stage cancers and benign polyps

4. Sphincter-Preserving Surgery

  • Transanal Minimally Invasive Surgery (TAMIS): Removes low rectal tumors without permanent ostomy
  • Intersphincteric Resection: Preserves anal function in ultra-low rectal cancers

5. Ostomy Care

  • Pre/Post-Op Counseling: Training for colostomy/ileostomy management
  • Custom Solutions: Advanced pouching systems and skin protection

Diagnosis & Staging

  • Colonoscopy: Gold standard for detecting polyps and tumors
  • Imaging: CT, MRI, or PET-CT to assess tumor spread
  • Genetic Testing: For Lynch syndrome (MSI/MMR testing)
