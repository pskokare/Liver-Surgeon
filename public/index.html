Chronic Liver Disease (CLD)

CLD is a long-term illness that damages your liver over time. It is commonly caused by alcohol abuse, several viral infections, fatty liver disease (non-alcoholic), etc.

Acute Liver Failure (ALF)

ALF degrades your liver’s function at a very short notice. It requires urgent medical attention without which the patient’s life may be threatened. It is caused mainly by liver specific viral infections, toxins, certain drug reactions/overdoses etc.

Fatty Liver

Excess fat accumulation in your liver can lead to inflammation, scarring, and liver cirrhosis if not treated in time. The good news is that if it is treated in time, the effects of fatty liver are completely reversible.

Liver Cirrhosis

Autoimmune Hepatitis

When a patient’s immune system mistakenly attacks their own liver, it leads to persistent and prolonged inflammation which if not identified and treated in time can lead to serious liver damage. We treat this condition mainly with immunosuppressive therapy.
