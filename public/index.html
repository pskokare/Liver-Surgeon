About Us

About Dr. Ashish George

Dr. Ashish George is an accomplished liver transplant surgeon, currently serving as the Principal Consultant and Unit Head in the Department of Liver Transplantation Surgery. With over 15 years of experience in liver transplantation and Hepato Pancreatic Biliary surgery (HPB), Dr. George has played a pivotal role in numerous life-saving surgeries, including pediatric and adult liver transplants, complex hepatobiliary surgeries, and innovative techniques like auxiliary partial orthotopic liver transplantation.

Read More

Credentials:

  • MBBS, MS (General Surgery) from the University College of Medical Sciences, Delhi
  • M.Ch. (Surgical Gastroenterology) from AIIMS, New Delhi
  • Post-Doctoral Fellowship in Liver Transplantation from TNMGR University, Chennai
  • Fellowship in Minimal Access Surgery from WLH
  • Active Member: Liver Transplant Society of India (LTSI), Association of Surgeons of India (ASI)

Experience:

With over 1000 successful liver transplant surgeries, including pediatric liver transplants, complex liver transplants, liver cancer surgeries, and more, Dr. George's expertise spans across the full spectrum of liver care. He has held senior role at renowned institutions such as Aakash Healthcare Superspecialty Hospital and BLK-Max Superspecialty Hospital, New Delhi.

Pediatric Liver Transplant

A specialized procedure for children with severe liver disease, focusing on providing life-saving transplants tailored to their unique medical needs.

Read More
Deceased Donor Liver Transplant

Involves transplanting a liver from a dead donor, offering hope to patients with liver failure who cannot find a suitable living donor.

Read More
Living Donor Liver Transplant

A procedure where a portion of a healthy person's liver is transplanted to a patient, allowing both the donor and recipient's liver to regenerate.

Read More
Split Liver Transplant

This technique involves dividing a donor's liver into two parts, enabling transplantation into two recipients, often used in pediatric and adult cases.

Read More
Dual Lobe Liver Transplant

A rare procedure where both lobes of a liver from a deceased donor are transplanted into two patients, typically an adult and a child, to maximize organ usage.

Read More
Combined Liver-Kidney Transplant

For patients suffering from both liver and kidney failure, this procedure combines a liver transplant with a kidney transplant to restore both organ functions.

Read More
Post Transplant Surveillance

Ongoing monitoring after a liver transplant to ensure proper organ function, detect early signs of rejection, and address any potential complications.

Read More
Immunosuppressive Therapy

Medication is prescribed to prevent organ rejection by suppressing the immune system, ensuring the transplanted liver is accepted and remains functional.

Read More
Chronic Liver Disease (CLD)

A long-term condition characterized by progressive liver damage, often caused by factors like alcohol use, viral infections, or non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD).

Read More
Acute Liver Failure (ALF)

A rapid decline in liver function, often due to infections, toxins, or underlying liver disease, requires urgent medical intervention and potentially a liver transplant.

Read More
Hepatitis A, B, C, D, E

Infections caused by different hepatitis viruses, affecting the liver in unique ways, ranging from mild to life-threatening, with varied treatment and prevention strategies.

Read More
Jaundice

A yellowing of the skin and eyes due to the buildup of bilirubin, often indicating liver dysfunction or bile duct obstruction.

Read More
Fatty Liver

An illness where excess fat accumulates in liver cells, which can lead to liver inflammation, scarring, and cirrhosis if left untreated.

Read More
Liver Cirrhosis

Advanced scarring of the liver results from chronic liver disease, impairing liver function and potentially leading to complications such as liver failure or cancer.

Read More
Autoimmune Hepatitis

A situation where the immune system mistakenly attacks the liver, causing inflammation and damage, often treated with immunosuppressive medications.

Read More
Alcoholic Hepatitis

Inflammation of the liver caused by excessive alcohol consumption, which can progress to cirrhosis and liver failure if not managed effectively.

Read More
Genetic Liver Disorders

Inherited conditions that affect liver function, including diseases like Wilson's disease and hemochromatosis, leading to abnormal storage of copper or iron in the liver.

Read More
Metabolic Liver Diseases

A group of liver disorders caused by metabolic abnormalities, such as Wilson's disease or NAFLD, which can lead to liver damage over time.

Read More
Cancer Surgeries

Surgical interventions aimed at removing liver tumors or cancerous growths, including treatments for hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) and metastatic liver cancer, to improve patient outcomes.

Watch Now
Advanced HPB Procedures

Complex surgeries related to Hepato Pancreatic Biliary (HPB) conditions, including liver, pancreas, and bile duct surgeries, are performed to treat conditions like pancreatic cancer, bile duct obstructions, and liver resections.

Watch Now
Diagnostics & Supportive Care

Comprehensive diagnostic services, including imaging and laboratory tests, coupled with supportive care to manage symptoms and improve the quality of life for patients with liver diseases or after surgery.

Why Choose Dr. Ashish George

Experience & Expertise

Dr. George has over 15 years of experience in liver transplantation and complex hepatobiliary surgeries. His ability to handle high-risk cases, including pediatric and adult liver transplants, is complemented by his role in setting international standards in liver transplant care. His expertise has earned him recognition and roles in prominent hospitals.

Commitment to Patient Care

Dr. George takes a holistic and patient-centered approach, ensuring comprehensive care before, during, and after the procedure. His team provides continuous support, making sure patients are satisfied at every step of their treatment.

Global Recognition

Dr. George's contributions extend beyond India, with collaborations in training and surgery abroad. He is frequently invited to global medical forums and has made noteworthy contributions to medical literature on liver transplantation and hepatobiliary surgery.
