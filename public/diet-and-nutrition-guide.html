Diet and Nutrition

Nutrition plays a critical role in your recovery after a liver transplant. At Liver Surgeons, Delhi’s leading liver care center, we provide personalized dietary guidelines to ensure that your new liver stays healthy, strong, and functional for life.A well-balanced diet helps you heal faster, strengthens your immune system, manages your weight, and reduces the risk of infections and complications.

Post-Transplant Nutrition

Key Dietary Guidelines After Liver Transplant

High-Protein Diet

Supports wound healing and immune recovery with eggs, fish, chicken, lean meats, lentils, and dairy products.

Low-Sodium Foods

Prevents fluid retention and kidney stress by avoiding processed foods, canned items, pickles, and salty snacks.

Manage Sugar Intake

Counteracts medication-induced blood sugar spikes by minimizing sweets, desserts, and refined carbohydrates.

Stay Hydrated

Supports kidney/liver function with proper hydration while observing fluid restrictions if prescribed.

Food Safety

Reduces infection risk through well-cooked foods, thorough washing, and avoiding raw/street foods.

Immune-Boosting Foods

Includes fresh fruits, leafy greens, nuts and seeds to strengthen immune defenses.

Limit Fat Intake

Prevents cardiovascular issues by choosing healthy fats and avoiding fried/processed foods.
