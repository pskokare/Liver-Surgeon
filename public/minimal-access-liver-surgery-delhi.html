What is Minimal Access Liver Surgery

Minimal access liver surgery uses small incisions and advanced technology to treat liver tumors, cysts, and other conditions, offering reduced recovery time and fewer complications compared to open surgery. At Liver Surgeons, Delhi’s premier center, we specialize in:

  • Laparoscopic Liver Resection: Removing tumors through tiny incisions.
  • Minimally Invasive Hepatectomy: Partial or full liver removal with precision.
  • Liver Cyst Fenestration: Draining or removing cysts without large scars.
  • Complex Procedures: Including single-incision and gasless techniques for select patients.

Surgical Procedures

  1. Laparoscopic Liver Resection
    • Technique: High-definition cameras and tools remove tumors via 3–4 small incisions.
    • Benefits: Less pain, minimal scarring, and reduced recovery time (5–7 days).
  2. Keyhole Liver Surgery (Hand-Assisted Laparoscopic)
    • Process: Combines a small hand-port with laparoscopic tools for complex tumors.
    • Use Cases: Large or challenging tumors requiring tactile feedback.
  3. Single-Incision Liver Surgery
    • Approach: The entire procedure is done through one tiny incision near the belly button.
    • Advantage: Virtually scarless results for cosmetic sensitivity.
  4. Minimally Invasive Liver Cyst Fenestration
    • Procedure: Drains or removes cysts using laparoscopic guidance.
    • Recovery: Patients are often discharged within 2–3 days.
  5. Gasless Laparoscopic Liver Resection
    • Innovation: Avoids CO₂ inflation, ideal for patients with cardiac or lung issues.
