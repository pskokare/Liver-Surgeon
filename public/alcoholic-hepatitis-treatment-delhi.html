What is Alcoholic Hepatitis?

Alcoholic hepatitis is acute liver inflammation caused by heavy alcohol use, ranging from mild to life-threatening. Key stages:

  • Mild: Reversible with alcohol cessation.
  • Severe: Jaundice, coagulopathy, and high mortality without urgent care.

Diagnosis & Severity Assessment

1. Maddrey’s Discriminant Function

  • Formula: Bilirubin + (4.6 × PT prolongation).
  • Score ≥32: Indicates severe alcoholic hepatitis needing corticosteroids.

2. MELD Score in Alcoholic Hepatitis

  • Predicts 30-day mortality. Scores ≥21 qualify for liver transplant evaluation.

3. Liver Biopsy

  • Confirms diagnosis and rules out cirrhosis or cancer.

Alcoholic Hepatitis Treatment

1. Immediate Alcohol Cessation

  • Inpatient Detox: Managed withdrawal under supervision.
  • Counseling: Psychologists and support groups to prevent relapse.

2. Corticosteroids for Alcoholic Hepatitis

  • Prednisolone: 40 mg/day for 28 days (reduces mortality in severe cases).
  • Lille Score: Assesses steroid response by day 7.

3. Liver Transplant Criteria

  • Eligibility: 6+ months of sobriety (exceptions for severe cases with MELD ≥25).
  • Process: NOTTO-listed priority for eligible patients.

4. Nutritional Support

  • High-protein, calorie-dense diets to combat malnutrition.
