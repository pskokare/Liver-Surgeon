Frequently Asked Questions

At Liver Surgeons Hospital, success rates are 98% for living donor and 90% for deceased donor transplants.

Costs range from ₹25-35 lakhs, depending on complexity. We offer EMI options.

We've successfully treated patients up to 70 years old, provided they meet fitness criteria.

Most patients resume normal activities in 3-6 months with lifelong immunosuppressive therapy.

Most patients remain in the hospital for 2–3 weeks post-surgery. This includes time in the ICU, followed by a regular ward stay for monitoring, medication adjustments, and rehabilitation.

Through the NOTTO network, a liver donor can be a living relative (18–55 years, blood group compatible, physically fit) or a deceased donor. Living donors typically donate a portion of their liver, regenerating within weeks.

Like all major surgeries, liver transplants carry risks such as bleeding, infection, graft rejection, or bile duct complications. However, with our advanced facilities and experienced surgeons, we maintain a <2% complication rate.
