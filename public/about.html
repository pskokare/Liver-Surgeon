About Us

Liver Surgeons Hospital

At Liver Surgeons, we are Delhi's premier multispecialty hospital dedicated exclusively to liver health, transplantation, and hepatobiliary sciences.

Established in 2015, our hospital has redefined liver care in India through cutting-edge treatments, patient-centric protocols, and global collaborations. With 1,000+ successful liver transplants and a 95% survival rate, we stand as a beacon of hope for patients battling complex liver diseases, cirrhosis, and acute liver failure.

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission

To provide accessible, world-class liver care through innovation, collaboration, and compassion. We aim to empower patients with life-saving treatments while advancing liver health through research and community education.

Our Vision

To become Asia's most trusted liver care destination by 2030, pioneering breakthroughs in transplantation, regenerative medicine, and AI-driven diagnostics.
