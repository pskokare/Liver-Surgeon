Comprehensive detection and management of liver conditions

Why Early Diagnosis Matters

  • Silent Progression: Many liver diseases show no symptoms until advanced stages.
  • Reversible Damage: Early detection of fibrosis or fatty liver can halt or reverse the disease.
  • Life-Saving Interventions: Timely diagnosis of tumors or cirrhosis enables curative treatment.

Comprehensive Diagnostics

  • Advanced Imaging: MRI, CT, and contrast-enhanced ultrasound for precise liver evaluation.
  • Pre-Transplant Workup: Assess liver function, vascular health, and eligibility for transplant.
  • Lab Testing: Interpret complex liver function tests (ALT, AST, INR, bilirubin) to guide treatment.

Supportive Care Services

  • Managing Complications: Ascites, hepatic encephalopathy, and portal hypertension.
  • Vaccination & Prevention: Hepatitis B/C screening and immunization.
  • Pain Management: Tailored protocols for chronic liver pain.
