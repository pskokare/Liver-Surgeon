What is a Combined Liver-Kidney Transplant?

A combined liver-kidney transplant is a complex dual organ transplant surgery where a patient receives both a liver and a kidney from the same donor in a single procedure. This lifesaving approach is critical for patients suffering from simultaneous liver and kidney failure due to conditions like:

  • Polycystic liver-kidney disease.
  • Chronic hepatitis with renal complications.
  • Metabolic disorders (e.g., primary hyperoxaluria).

Who Needs a Dual Organ Transplant?

Eligibility Criteria

  • Liver & Kidney Failure: Irreversible damage to both organs (e.g., cirrhosis + stage 5 CKD)
  • Metabolic Disorders: Diseases like primary hyperoxaluria that damage both organs
  • Failed Single Organ Transplant: Patients with recurrent organ failure post-transplant

Symptoms Requiring Evaluation

  • Jaundice + severe edema/swelling
  • High creatinine (>5 mg/dL) with elevated liver enzymes
  • Frequent dialysis dependency + ascites

The Transplant Process

Step 1: Evaluation

Dual Organ Assessment:

  • Liver biopsy + GFR testing
  • Cardiac/pulmonary fitness checks
  • MELD Score ≥30 + eGFR <30 mL/min for priority

Step 2: Donor Matching

  • Deceased Donor: Organs from same donor ensure compatibility
  • Living Donor (Rare): Requires ethical approval for two donors

Step 3: Surgery

  • 12-18 hour procedure: Led by Dr. Ashish George
  • Key challenge: Coordinating two organ implantations

Step 4: Post-Transplant Care

  • Tailored immunosuppression for both organs
  • 3-month rehab with specialists
