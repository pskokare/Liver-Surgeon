What Are Hepatology Conditions?

Hepatology is the branch of medicine dedicated to studying, diagnosing, and treating diseases of the liver, gallbladder, and bile ducts. The liver, one of the body’s most crucial organs, performs critical functions like detoxification, protein synthesis, and bile production. When compromised by conditions such as cirrhosis, hepatitis, or genetic disorders, it can lead to life-threatening complications. At Liver Surgeons, Delhi’s premier hepatology clinic, we leverage cutting-edge technology like MRI elastography and FibroScan® alongside a team of globally trained specialists to address both common and rare liver diseases. Advanced Liver Health Diagnosis

At Liver Surgeons, Delhi’s premier liver care center, we deploy cutting-edge diagnostic tools to detect and monitor liver conditions with unmatched precision.

1. Liver Function Tests (LFTs)

  • ALT & AST: Measure liver inflammation (elevated in hepatitis or fatty liver).
  • ALP & GGT: Identify bile duct blockages (high in gallstones or cholestasis).
  • Bilirubin: Assesses jaundice severity (linked to liver dysfunction or hemolysis).
  • Why It Matters: LFTs are the first step in uncovering hidden liver damage, guiding further evaluation.

2. Advanced Imaging

  • FibroScan®: A painless, non-invasive test to stage liver fibrosis/cirrhosis in 5 minutes.
  • MRI Elastography: Maps liver stiffness and fat content, ideal for tracking NAFLD progression.
  • CT Angiography: Detects vascular issues (e.g., portal hypertension) or liver tumors.
  • Why It Matters: Replace invasive biopsies in 50% of cases, ensuring faster, safer diagnoses.

3. Ultrasound-Guided Biopsy

  • Purpose: Confirms cirrhosis, autoimmune hepatitis, or liver cancer.
  • Process: 20-minute procedure under local anesthesia; results in 48 hours.

Why It Matters: Gold standard for diagnosing complex cases like metabolic liver diseases or genetic disorders.
